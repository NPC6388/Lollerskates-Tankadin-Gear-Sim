## (a) Reddit self-post — r/classicwow or r/wowclassic

**Title:** I made a free Prot Paladin addon that shows your uncrittable/uncrushable/EHP live and builds tank sets from your bags (TBC Anniversary)

**Body:**

I tank on a Prot Paladin on the TBC Anniversary realms and got tired of alt-tabbing to a
spreadsheet to check whether a gear swap kept me uncrittable and uncrushable. So I built an
addon for it. Sharing it here in case it's useful to other tankadins — it's free and
open-source, no strings.

**Tankadin Gear Sim** (`/tgs`) does two things:

- **Live tank readout** off your character sheet — uncrittable ✓/✗ with surplus, the
  avoidance breakdown, uncrushable status + crush surplus, armor DR, physical EHP, spell
  power, and block value. It recomputes as you swap pieces, and you can toggle "assume Holy
  Shield" and "assume Kings + Mark of the Wild" so the numbers match a real pull.
- **In-game optimizer** — an Optimize tab reads your bags and bank and builds four tuned
  sets (Raid Threat / Survival / AOE Trash / Balanced), each enforcing the tank caps, with
  per-goal EHP↔Threat and minimum-HP sliders. A minimap button opens an ItemRack-style
  flyout to preview and equip any set in one click (it keeps your gems and enchants).

There's a companion website too — you can export your gear from the addon and upload it for
the same optimizer plus a gem/enchant solver and phase/BiS planning. All the math comes
from Lollerskate's TBC Prot Paladin tanking guide, and the in-game engine is parity-tested
against the site so the numbers agree.

Fair warning: it's a **working beta (v0.8.x)**. The core readout and optimizer are solid in
my own raids, but I'd love more eyes on it — **bug reports and feedback are very welcome**,
especially if a stat reads wrong on an item or a set comes out looking off. Tell me the item
and I'll get it fixed.
