P3–P5 Encounter Gearing

TBC Protection Paladin — when to leave your threat set at the door.

Once you're uncrittable and uncrushable, tanking TBC is a threat race — you gear for threat and hold it from Phase 2 all the way to the Sunwell. Only a handful of fights make you deviate. This is the short list of when, why, and which set to bring.

The default: your threat set

Two hard gates come first, and they are non-negotiable — the sim enforces both:

  • Uncrittable — 490 defense skill (or the resilience equivalent) so a raid boss can't land a critical hit on you. Below this you take spike damage that gets tanks killed.
  • Uncrushable — dodge + parry + block + miss (with Holy Shield up) ≥ 102.4%, which pushes crushing blows off the attack table.

Clear both and every remaining stat point goes to threat: spell power, spell hit, expertise, hit, and block value. This threat set — the sim's Raid Threat and Balanced presets — is what you wear for the large majority of bosses in Black Temple, Hyjal, Zul'Aman and the Sunwell. You only step off it for the fights below.

Rule of thumb: don't swap gear because a boss hits hard. Swap only when a mechanic changes the math — strips your avoidance, or removes block/threat from the equation entirely. There are exactly three of those in P3–P5.

Phase 3 Black Temple & Mount Hyjal

Illidan Stormrage — the Shear check

Illidan's Shear is a single melee special that cannot miss. Because it can't be missed, miss rating does nothing for it — the only way to fully avoid it is with the rest of the table: dodge + parry + block (+ Holy Shield) ≥ 101.8%. That's a touch under the normal 102.4% crush cap, precisely because the miss slice is off the table.

Bring the Illidan preset. It re-targets the uncrushable gate to 101.8% measured on dodge/parry/block only (miss excluded), then leans any surplus back into threat. If your gear can't reach 101.8% the set is honestly flagged illegal — that's a gear check, not a bug.

Everything else in P3 (Council, Mother, Bloodboil, the Hyjal bosses) is a normal threat fight — wear your threat set.

Phase 4 Zul'Aman

No special avoidance mechanics here — threat set throughout. What Phase 4 really is, for a tankadin, is an itemization jump from the Badge of Justice vendor. The P4 badge wave adds several tank plate pieces worth chasing while you wait on drops — they're in the sim's “also viable” lists so you can “pretend I own this” and see the delta:

  • Chestguard of the Stoic Guardian — three-socket avoidance + spell-power chest.
  • Iron-tusk Girdle — defense + dodge belt.
  • Bracers of the Ancient Phalanx — expertise-leaning threat bracer.

Phase 5 Sunwell Plateau

Sunwell Radiance — avoidance gets gutted

The Sunwell aura, Sunwell Radiance, reduces your chance to be missed by 5% and your dodge by 20%. That guts avoidance-stacking across the whole tier. The saving grace: of the Sunwell bosses, only Lady Sacrolash actually crushes, and a solid core survival set already covers her — so the crush gate is relaxed for the tier. You stop chasing the 102.4% wall you can no longer reach and instead lean EHP while keeping the avoidance you can.

Bring the Sunwell preset for the general tier: crush gate relaxed, EHP-focused but avoidance-retaining, with the Radiance-adjusted numbers shown for the Sacrolash reference.

Brutallus — the stamina wall

Brutallus is the one fight in TBC that throws out the usual playbook. Meteor Slash & melee land for roughly ~12k physical, there are no adds, and block value is worthless against hits that size. Threat is trivial. What keeps you standing is raw Effective HP — a big health pool behind your armor. Aim for a >20k HP pool and take every stamina and armor point you can.

Bring the Brutallus preset — a max-EHP set (crush gate relaxed, no threat weight). This is the one place the solver will pick a pure-stamina piece over a spell-power one: e.g. Iron Gauntlets of the Maiden gemmed for stamina beats your threat gloves here, even though they'd lose on every other fight.

Everything else in the Sunwell

Kalecgos, Felmyst, the Eredar Twins (Alythess), M'uru and Kil'jaeden are threat/EHP-lean fights with no gear swap — Radiance is in effect, so you're on the Sunwell set, but there's no per-boss change beyond that. New P5 badge legs/belt/chest are in the sim's lists to plug gaps (Inscribed Legplates of the Aldor, Chestplate of Stoicism, and friends).

Consumables (on you, not the sim)

The optimizer scores gear — it deliberately doesn't model consumables or procs. On the deviation fights above, these are yours to bring and they matter:

  • Flask of Fortification — +500 HP, +10 defense, all night. The baseline raid-tank flask.
  • Stamina food — Fisherman's Feast (+30 sta) or Dirge's Kickin' Chimaerok Chops.
  • Scroll of Protection — cheap armor before a physical pull.
  • Ironshield Potion — big temporary armor for a spike window (e.g. a Brutallus burn).
  • Nightmare Seed — the P5 panic button; an instant self-heal to survive an over-lap.

Rule of thumb: on Brutallus, stack everything HP/armor; on Illidan, the flask's +10 defense and any avoidance food help you clear the 101.8% Shear gate.

The short version

  • Get uncrittable + uncrushable, then gear threat. That set carries P2→P5.
  • Illidan (P3): Shear can't miss — bring the set built to 101.8% dodge/parry/block.
  • Sunwell (P5): Radiance guts dodge; only Sacrolash crushes, so lean EHP, keep avoidance.
  • Brutallus (P5): pure stamina wall, >20k HP, block value is dead weight.
  • Everything else: threat set. Don't swap for “hits hard” — swap for a mechanic that changes the math.
