About the ecosystem

Addon v0.8.45

Start in the game — the addon does the heavy lifting

Tankadin Gear Sim (/tgs) is the front door. It gives you a live tank readout off your character sheet, an in-game Optimize tab that builds four tuned sets — Raid Threat, Survival, AOE Trash, and Balanced — each with its own EHP↔Threat and Min-HP sliders, a minimap flyout to preview and equip any set in one click (gems and enchants kept), and one-click export to this site.

↓ Download the addon (ZIP) Source on GitHub

New in v0.8.45: gear tooltips now show Threat and EHP deltas vs the item you'd replace. The threat number is spell-power-equivalent — the sim's threat weights (spell power + spell hit + expertise + hit + block value) applied to the stat change, so a hit/expertise piece counts, not just raw spell power.

  1. Install

    Unzip into Interface\AddOns, enable TankadinGearSim, and type /tgs.

  2. Live readout

    Uncrittable / uncrushable status, avoidance, armor DR and EHP — recomputed as you swap gear.

  3. Optimize in-game

    Solve four sets from your bags and bank, then equip one from the minimap flyout.

  4. Export

    Open your bank, hit Export, then /reload to flush it to disk.

  5. Plan on the site

    Upload the file below for the gem/enchant solver, phase scoping, and BiS planning.

Go deeper on this website

This site runs the same optimizer (the Lua port is parity-tested against the JS) and adds what only makes sense with room to think: a full gem & enchant solver, content-phase scoping, and BiS “pretend I own this” planning so you can see what to chase next. Everything runs in your browser — your gear never leaves it.

All the math is transcribed from Lollerskate’s TBC Prot Paladin Tanking Guide — the first-principles foundation every constant and stat weight is built on.

Use your own gear — install the addon & upload

⬇ Download the TankadinGearSim addon (ZIP)

  1. Install: unzip it into your WoW Interface\AddOns folder — e.g. World of Warcraft\_anniversary_\Interface\AddOns\ — so you end up with an AddOns\TankadinGearSim\ folder. Restart WoW, and at the character screen open AddOns and make sure TankadinGearSim is checked.
  2. Open your bank (so banked gear is included), then run /tgs and click the Export tab. Then type /reload to write the file to disk.
  3. Upload the SavedVariables file below: World of Warcraft\_anniversary_\WTF\Account\<your account>\SavedVariables\TankadinGearSim.lua

Sixty Upgrades stat weights

The stat-weight scales this sim uses — paste them into Sixty Upgrades (or any Pawn-compatible planner) to rank gear yourself.

How to use these in Sixty Upgrades
  1. Pick the scale that matches your goal (single-target threat, AOE, survival, balanced) and click Copy weights (JSON).
  2. Open your character at sixtyupgrades.com, go to Custom Stat Weights, and paste the JSON. Sixty Upgrades will now rank gear & enchants by it.
  3. Caps come first. Weights don't enforce the hard gates — reach uncrittable (490 Defense, or 5.6% crit reduction) and uncrushable (102.4% combined avoidance with Holy Shield) before maximizing by weight.
